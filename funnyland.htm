Funnyland is the island that the Funny Guys call home. The island's geography is completely random at most. A desert next to a jungle...
A barren mountain range next to a bustling metropolis... you get the idea. Obviously, the FG's house is located in the suburbs of Funnytown, where
Element first started his journey. Two states have since been banished from Funnyland: Discipland and Not-Funnyworld. As seen on the map, they 
were ripped clean out of the original continent, and the space left was named Outcast Canal. Humortopica, the capital of Funnyland, is a city of 
gambling, big money, and car accidents taken to the next level. Funnyland as a whole is slightly north of the equator, though no one is sure where.
