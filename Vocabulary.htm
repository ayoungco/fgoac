Vocabulary

Talk like a real Funny Guy Pro! (Highlight if you can't see it)

More terms will be added as the comics progress.

Koven Wars- A chain of intense battles fought to preserve the Funny Guy name, our reputation. (SERIES; Comics #18 to #40)

Funny Guy Revolution- When the Funny Guy Universe was threatened by Koven,who would become an extremely annoying enemy of the FGOAC in years to come. The Revolution would eventually lead to the beginning of the Koven Wars. (SERIES; Comics #9-#17)

Gas Super Soaker- A normal Super Soaker filled with gasoline to become a flamethrower and/or rocket launcher (*DO NOT TRY AT HOME!*).

Pee Soda- No, it is NOT real pee. It is a lemonade-soda type beverage favored by all the Funny Guys for its rejuvenating qualities (and flavor).

Poo Cake- And no, this is NOT what you think it is. It is a cake with only a few ingredients...Sugar, sugar, sugar, chocolate, milk, eggs, and flour fortified with sugar. Potty-P recently stopped serving it because every time he served it to Funny Guy, he blew a hole in the roof because of "hyperness".

Vomit Pizza- Again, this is NOT what you think it is. It's just a pizza with extra tomato sauce in it.

Anti-Anti-Monster Potion- A strange concoction created by the "Evil" Scientist that, to his protest, MAKES monsters.

Gaseous Odorous- The technical word for fart.

Ford Excursion- One of the two vehicles driven by the Funny Guys.

Hummer H2- A powerful SUV driven by the Funny Guys.

Randy Panda Doll- A defective storytime toy that gave the FGs a hard time in Comic #199: The Revenge of the Toys.

Particle Accelerator- An electric cannon, Target's favorite.

Deodorant Bomb- A weapon used by Razor to counteract Repair Man's "Pit Beam".

AC (Armored Core)- Large Combat machines used by the Funny Guys to fight evil. There's actually a video game for them!

Chuckie Pea's- Jr.'s favorite restaurant in all of Funnyland.

B.O.O.M.S.- Stands for: BORED OUT OF MY SANITY!

Robo Mower- A robotic mower that almost defeated Ultimate Fusion FG.

Moose Hunter- Target's favorite video game (And when I say favorite I MEAN favorite).
