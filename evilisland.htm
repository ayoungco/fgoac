Northwest of Not-Funnyworld, there is an island named Evil Island. Most of the tiny space is occupied by three large areas known as the Empties.
These Empties are complete and absolute nothing. Yep. Void. Zero. Nada. Zilch. The land is perfectly flat, a glossy black color with a smoky look to it. 
The sky is also black with purple clouds, giving the whole area a mysterious look to it. Strangely, any "city" located in any Empty is just a statue with 
words on it. An FG hideout is located here, but it was abandoned since the Koven Wars. The Evilies converse in the Dragon's Lair, making it their 
headquarters.
