FGRPG Demo Online!

It's finally here! The FGRPG's (Funny Guy Role-Playing Game, not Funny Guy Rocket-Propelled Grenade!) demo release is online and ready for download! The story: You are Funny Guy, starting the adventure in Gas Town. The Humorless have terrorized the continent of Funnyland, and you must stop them and their leader before they sap all the humor and color out of the world! You gather the Funny Guys, level up, and gain new skills on your way across Funnyland. No corner of the continent is left hidden to you and your comrades (except in the demo), so go get 'em!
