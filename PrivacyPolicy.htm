FGOAC Privacy Policy

 

FGOAC.com is commited to the safety of its costumers and viewers, and will not sell your personal 
information to other companies. Due to US laws requiring the safety of individuals under 13 years of 
age online, we will not accept information from minors unless they have their parent's/guardian's 
permission. For products ordered online, a mailing address is required.This address means nothing to 
FGOAC, besides for shipping purposes. Our site does distribute cookies. Our hit counter on the 
Home Page sends out an ID cookie, and our forums send out a "Remember Me" cookie that allows
members to login automatically-- this is optional. If you are banned or suspended from the forums,
the software records your IP address and I, as an administrator, can view it. If you have any questions 
regarding our privacy policy, you can send e-mail to adamyoung@suscom.net adam@ayoung.co.
