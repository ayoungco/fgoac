Elemental Funny Guy
Element is the first of all the Funny Guys, evolving
from a mere comedian to a battle-oriented Repair
Man trapper. No one knows where he originated,
but his secret might be revealed Spring 2005...
The two swords that Element carries used to have 
razor-sharp barbs, but he recently found that they
hinder him more than they help him. So he had 
them removed. Element, as his name suggests, is
very good at using the forces of nature to crush
his opponents.
