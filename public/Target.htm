Target Funny Guy

a chance to shoot stuff. He is also eagerly
awaiting the shooting range that is to be 
built within Funnytown, so he doesn't have to 
drive 1,000 miles every day to get to the one
in Sports World. Target has countless guns 
and cannons, so he always has the right one.
