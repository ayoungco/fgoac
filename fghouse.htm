The FGs live in this 6-floor mansion, and usually can spend months inside without coming out, mainly 
because there is so much stuff you can do inside. Attorney works on computers in the attic, Target 
and Olympian work on the vehicles, Razor and Element watch TV, and Junior plays in his room. The
FGs also get information for missions in the Briefing Room, and they can get to the cars just by going 
down the elevator if they need to. Originally, the house was a trailer, which Element fixed up and 
began to live in.
