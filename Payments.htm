Payments Page
On this page, you can calculate S&H and other things on your purchase so there's no hassle 
for you or us.

Comics (Single)

1) There are no shipping and handling fees associated with single comic purchases under
3 in quantity, however: If you purchase three or more single comics, the shipping cost is
added ($0.05 per comic).

2) Read the RETURN POLICY for information on single comic warranties.

Comic Books

1) Shipping and handling for one comic book covers all of the comic books included in your 
purchase (Highest fee counts).

2) Warranty- Same as single comics (see #2 above).

Apparel

1) Shipping and handling fees for apparel puchases under $30.00 are $3.00. This covers 
the entire apparel purchase. If apparel purchases are over $30.00, the fee increases
$1.00 for every item.

2) Warranty- Apparel can be returned within 15 days after purchase

Games

1) Shipping and handling- $5.00

2) Warranty- Can be returned 10 days after purchase.

Posters

1) S&H- None

2) Warranty- None

Terms

The time of your purchase is when we recieve the money. We will contact you at that point.
