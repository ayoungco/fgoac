Timetravel Funny Guy
Timet is one of the most popular FGs, mainly because he can- what do you think!? Yes, he can manipulate time, Mr. Obvious. His staffs have VCR controls on them, so he can pause time, rewind time, fast-forward time, stop time, and of course play time. Timetravel can also jump back and forth between comics, giving him the advantage over his challengers.
