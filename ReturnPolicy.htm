FGOAC Return Policy

 

As our company grows bigger, we overtake certain responsibilities that require public attention. We
have noticed the potential DANGER to our comics and profits if an individual tries to fool us. After a few 
meetings with FGOAC as a whole, we have come to an agreement: If the consumer has purchased the wrong 
strip, they may return the comic in trade for their money or another comic, as long as the price is the same. If, 
by any reason, the purchased comic's packaging envelope is opened (i.e., ripped, torn, cut, etc.), the buyer will 
not receive their refund. Any other merchandise distributed by the FGOAC has a warranty and/or return guidelines 
that are clearly displayed. Inform us ahead of time if you want to return something. Furthermore, The 2004 
Copyright: Funny Guy is property of the Funny Guy Organized Association of Comics (FGOAC).Use of F.G. 
logos, traits, and/or indicia is strictly prohibited without consent of the FGOAC.
